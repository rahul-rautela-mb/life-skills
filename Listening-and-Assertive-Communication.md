

## **1. Steps/Strategies for Active Listening**

* Pay full attention.
* Maintain eye contact and good body language.
* Don’t interrupt.
* Ask any doubts.
* Repeat what you heard.
* Summarize the key points.


## **2. Key Points of Reflective Listening**

* Listen to both the words and the feelings.
* Repeat what the person said to check understanding.
* Try to understand their emotions.
* Confirm that you understand what they meant.
* Avoid judging and focus on clarity.

---

## **3. Obstacles in my listening process**

* I get distracted easily.
* Sometimes I think of my reply instead of listening.
* I make assumptions too fast.
* I multitask during conversations.
* My thoughts sometimes block my listening.

---

## **4. What I can do to improve my listening:**

* Stay focused and avoid multitasking.
* Wait for the other person to finish.
* Ask doubts.
* Teach back what I understood.
* Be patient.

---

## **5. When I switch to Passive communication:**

* When I want to avoid conflict.
* When I’m tired.
* When I don’t want to disappoint others.

---

## **6. When I switch to Aggressive communication:**

* When I’m frustrated.
* When I feel I’m not being heard.
* When things feel urgent.

---

## **7. When I switch to Passive-Aggressive communication:**

* When I feel upset but don’t want to say it directly.
* When I use sarcasm or ignore someone.
* When I avoid confrontation but still feel angry.

---

## **8. How I can make my communication Assertive:**

* Use clear sentences.
* Say what I need using “I” statements.
* Respect others while conveying my views.
* Listen first, then respond.
* Set boundaries.
