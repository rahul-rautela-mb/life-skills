# Focus Management
---

## **1: What is Deep Work?**

Deep Work means **focusing without distraction** on a demanding task.
It involves:

* Full concentration
* No multitasking
* No distractions
* Working in a mentally challenging zone
* Producing high-quality results in less time

---

## **2: How to do Deep Work properly**

* Schedule deep work sessions deliberately
* Remove all distractions (phone away, block websites, quiet space)
* Decide on a specific start and end time
* Set clear goals for each deep work session
* Prioritize your most important tasks for deep work
* Train your ability to focus
---

## **3: How to implement these principles in daily life?**

* Block 1–2 hours of distraction-free time in your calendar
* Keep your phone in another room during focused work
* Plan the top 1–3 important tasks each day
* Work in short initial sessions and build up
* Turn off notifications and close unnecessary tabs
* End each day by planning tomorrow’s deep work goals

---

## **4: Dangers of Social Media**

* Reduces attention span
* Affects sleep quality
* Increases anxiety and FOMO
* Wastes time through endless scrolling
* Makes focused work harder
* Can distort reality and emotional balance

---

## **Summary of Diffused Mode Section**

Diffused mode is when the brain relaxes and forms creative connections.
You access it by:

* Meditation
* Silent walks
* Quiet sitting or lying down
* Mindful chores
* Time away from screens

---

## **Getting Started on Difficult Tasks — Key Takeaway**

Procrastination happens because you avoid uncomfortable emotions (fear, boredom, frustration).
The solution:

1. Work for **just 30 minutes**
2. Take a short break
3. Tell yourself: *“I can do this. This emotion is normal.”*
4. Repeat and gradually increase time
