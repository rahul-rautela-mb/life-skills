
# **Tiny Habits**

### 1. In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The idea that tiny actions—like doing just 2 push-ups—can grow into real habits.
It shows motivation isn’t the key; **making the habit very small and easy** is what makes it stick.


### 2. How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P?

B = MAP means:

* **M = Motivation**
* **A = Ability (how easy it is)**
* **P = Prompt (reminder)**

You make habits easier by focusing on **Ability** → make the habit tiny, simple, and connected to a clear prompt so you don’t depend on motivation.


### 3. Why is it important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of the habit?

Because **emotion creates habits**.
A little celebration gives your brain a reward signal, which strengthens the habit and makes you want to repeat it.


### 4. In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The idea that getting just **1% better every day** leads to massive long-term improvement.
Small gains compound into big results.

### 5. What is the book’s perspective about Identity?

It says habits should be built around **who you want to become**, not around goals.
Every habit is a “vote” for your identity (e.g., “I am a reader,” not “I want to read”).


### 6. Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do.

Make it easy by:

* Reducing friction
* Starting small
* Setting up a supportive environment
* Using habit stacking (adding new habit after an existing one)


### 7. Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do.

Make it harder by:

* Adding friction
* Making it inconvenient
* Removing cues and triggers
* Adding barriers like logging out or blocking apps

### 8. Pick one habit that you would like to do more of. What steps can you take to make the cue obvious or the habit more attractive or easy and/or response satisfying?

**Example Habit: Reading**

* Put the book where you will see it
* Start with just 1–2 pages
* Pair it with something enjoyable
* Celebrate afterward

### 9. Pick one habit that you would like to eliminate or do less of. What steps can you take to make the cue invisible or the process unattractive or hard or the response unsatisfying?

**Example Habit: Reducing social media**

* Keep the phone in another room
* Make apps harder to reach
* Turn screen grayscale
* Add limits or accountability
