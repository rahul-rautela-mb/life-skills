# Grit and Growth Mindset
## Grit

Grit means staying committed to long-term goals with passion and not giving up even when things get difficult.

---

## Growth Mindset

A growth mindset is the belief that abilities can improve with effort, practice, and learning, whereas a fixed mindset holds that talent is unchangeable.

---

## Internal Locus of Control

Internal Locus of Control means believing that your actions shape your results.
The key point: focus on what you can control—your effort, habits, and choices—to stay motivated.

---

## How to Build a Growth Mindset

* Embrace challenges
* Learn from feedback
* Celebrate others’ success
* Replace negative self-talk
* Set learning goals
* Accept failure as part of learning
* Practice consistently

---

## Ideas to Build a Growth Mindset

Take responsibility for your learning, stay with problems until solved, use confusion and errors as learning signals, research before asking for help, understand concepts before coding, stay calm and confident, follow a simple process (relax → focus → research → code → repeat), and improve code step-by-step (work → readable → modular → efficient).
