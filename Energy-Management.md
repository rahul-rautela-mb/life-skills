# Energy Management

---

## **1: Calm Quadrant — What relaxes you?**

* Taking a slow walk
* Listening to calming music
* Doing yoga
* Meditation or deep breathing
* Spending time in nature
* Taking a warm bath
* Sitting quietly without actions

---

## **2: When do you enter the Stress quadrant?**


* Starting something new 
* Deadlines or time-pressure situations
* High-intensity workouts
* Multitasking or handling many responsibilities
* Lack of clarity about next steps
* Social pressure

---

## **3: How do you know you’re in the Excitement quadrant?**

* High energy
* positive emotion
* Feeling motivated
* Looking forward to a task
* You feel challenged but not overwhelmed
* Creativity flows easily
* You want to share your experience with others

---

# **4. Sleep Is Your Superpower**


* Sleep restores brain and body
* Lack of sleep affects learning and memory
* Sleep strengthens the immune system
* Sleep loss increases risk of disease
* Deep sleep cleans the toxins from brain
* Good sleep improves emotional stability

---

## **5: What can you do to sleep better?**

* Maintain consistent sleep and wake times
* Avoid screens 1–2 hours before bed
* Keep your room cool, dark and quiet
* Get morning sunlight exposure
* Light physical movement during the day
* Limit morning naps or keep them short

---

## **6: Brain-Changing Benefits of Exercise**

* Exercise improves mood by releasing beneficial brain chemicals
* Regular movement boosts learning, memory, and attention
* Exercise stimulates the growth of new brain cells
* Physical activity protects the brain from aging
* Exercise reduces stress and anxiety
* Movement increases energy and mental clarity

---

## **7: Steps you can take to exercise more**

* Schedule a specific time each day
* Start small: 5–10 minutes of walking
* Choose activities you enjoy
* Keep workout clothes easily accessible
* Track habits or progress
* Join a group or exercise with a friend
* Keep goals simple and realistic
